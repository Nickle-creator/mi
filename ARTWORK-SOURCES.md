# Added Artwork Sources

These reproductions are bundled as local WebP files; the website references only the filenames and does not load the images from remote URLs.

- Katsushika Hokusai, *Under the Wave off Kanagawa (The Great Wave)*, ca. 1830–32 — local file: `great-wave-lite.webp`. The Metropolitan Museum of Art’s object record identifies the work and labels it Public Domain; its collection API marks `isPublicDomain: true` and provides the source image. [The Met object record](https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/45434) · [The Met Collection API](https://collectionapi.metmuseum.org/public/collection/v1/objects/45434)
- Georges Seurat, *A Sunday on La Grande Jatte — 1884*, 1884–86 — local file: `la-grande-jatte-lite.webp`. The Art Institute of Chicago identifies the painting and marks its image CC0/Public Domain. [Artwork record](https://www.artic.edu/artworks/27992/a-sunday-on-la-grande-jatte-1884) · [Open Access policy](https://www.artic.edu/open-access/open-access-images)
- Mary Cassatt, *The Child’s Bath*, 1893 — local file: `childs-bath-lite.webp`. The Art Institute of Chicago identifies the painting and marks its image CC0/Public Domain. The bundled high-resolution reproduction is the Wikimedia Commons Google Art Project file, marked Public Domain as a faithful reproduction of a two-dimensional public-domain work. [Art Institute artwork record](https://www.artic.edu/artworks/111442/the-child-s-bath) · [Commons file and license](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Mary_Cassatt_-_The_Child%27s_Bath_-_Google_Art_Project.jpg) · [Art Institute Open Access policy](https://www.artic.edu/open-access/open-access-images)
- Claude Monet, *Cliff Walk at Pourville*, 1882 — local file: `cliff-walk-pourville-lite.webp`. The Art Institute of Chicago’s artwork record gives the title, artist, date, and CC0 Public Domain Designation. The bundled reproduction is the Wikimedia Commons Google Art Project file, identified as a faithful reproduction of a two-dimensional public-domain work. [Art Institute artwork record](https://www.artic.edu/artworks/14620/cliff-walk-at-pourville) · [Commons file and license](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Claude_Monet_-_Cliff_Walk_at_Pourville_-_Google_Art_Project.jpg)
- Camille Pissarro, *Landscape, Ile-de-France*, 1873 — local file: `landscape-ile-de-france-lite.webp`. The National Gallery of Art’s record identifies the work and states that its image is free and in the public domain. [NGA artwork record and image download](https://www.nga.gov/artworks/164950-landscape-ile-de-france)

The Met describes Open Access public-domain images as available for unrestricted commercial and noncommercial use. The Art Institute states that images bearing its CC0 Public Domain Designation are available for use without permission or fee; it requests the artist, title, date, and museum in the reproduction caption. The site’s cutout captions carry the artist/title/date; this file records museum and image-source credits.
